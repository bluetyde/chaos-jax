# Papers needed from the owner (task H0.8)

Everything here was paywalled, a book, or not reachable on 2026-10-26. Put copies in a folder outside this repo (licences are unclear). In priority order: the first three decide whether the word "record" can be used.

1. **Rössler, O. E., "An equation for hyperchaos", Physics Letters A 71, 155-157 (1979).** The original 4D system and its exponents. Why: the plan's positive control and the first hyperchaotic flow; only read through a later paper that prints the equations. The publisher page returned HTTP 403. DOI not checked by me.
2. **Sprott, J. C., *Elegant Chaos: Algebraically Simple Chaotic Flows*, World Scientific (2010), section 6.7 "Hyperchaotic systems".** Why: the one place most likely to list the simplest known hyperchaotic flows with term counts, written by the author of the term-counting method. Link: https://worldscientific.com/doi/10.1142/7183
3. **Sheet, A. T., 4D hyperchaotic system based on Sprott B (2022); venue not identified.** Why: Yu et al. 2023 say it has seven terms and two nonlinear terms, so it ties the 7-term row of the table. I could only see it cited in Yu et al., Mathematics 11(3), 701 (2023), ref. [58].
4. **Chen, D., Chen, H., Ma, X., Long, Y., "只含一个非线性项的超混沌系统及其控制比较" (Hyperchaos system with only one nonlinear term and comparative study of its chaotic control), Journal of Computer Applications 30(8), 2045-2048 (2010).** Why: task H0.4 names it; the journal page has the abstract only and the PDF link does not work. Link: https://www.joca.cn/EN/Y2010/V30/I8/2045
5. **Stankevich, N. V., Kazakov, A. O., Gonchenko, S. V., "Scenarios of hyperchaos occurrence in 4D Rössler system", Chaos (2020).** Why: likely source of Lyapunov exponent values for the 1979 parameters (a = 0.25, b = 3, c = 0.5, d = 0.05), which no text I could read prints. Found only as a citation on a conference poster; exact volume and DOI not checked.
6. **Wilczak, D., Serrano, S., Barrio, R., "When chaos meets hyperchaos: 4D Rössler model", Physics Letters A 379, 2300-2305 (2015); DOI 10.1016/j.physleta.2015.07.035 (as given in a search result).** Why: the earlier proof for the classical parameters. The 2016 SIAM paper by the same authors was read in part (open copy), this one was not looked at.
